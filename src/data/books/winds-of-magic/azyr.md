# Azyr & the Lore of Heavens

## Overview

Azyr, the Blue Wind, is light and insubstantial, drifting in the upper atmosphere before dissipating. It governs weather, storms, and the movements of the stars, and is intimately tied to fate and prophecy — Azyr is the one Wind said to flow outside strict linear time, offering glimpses of possible futures. Casters gain +1 SL to Channelling on high towers or hills, rising to +2 SL atop the highest peaks or aboard a flying device.

## The Celestial Order

- **Symbol:** Comet, Crescent, Star. **Focus:** Inspiration, Imagination, Exploration. **Title:** Astromancer.
- Wizards of the Celestial Order (the Fourth Lore of Magic) wear blue robes and study Astromancy: reading the night sky, foretelling disaster, and commanding storms and lightning in battle.
- Their college occupies sixteen glass-domed towers in Altdorf's Hexxerbezrik, concealed from casual passersby by minor magic. Its most famous engine is the **Hurricanum**, a mobile war-carriage mounting an orrery built around an Orb of Sorcery, used to call down storms and grant foresight to nearby troops.
- Astromancers are valued by the Imperial Navy (for weather prediction) and the Cult of Verena (shared scholarship), but viewed with suspicion by the Cult of Morr, who worry augury usurps Morr's domain over fate.

## Arcane Marks of Azyr

A d10 table of minor mutation-like quirks for characters heavily touched by Azyr — e.g. eyes turning pale glowing blue (-1 SL Fellowship), a thin airy voice that can't shout, becoming slightly transparent (-5 Strength), or gaining +5 Intelligence/-10 Fellowship from an absent-minded, "head in the clouds" demeanour. Rolling a 10 grants a visible rune and the Suffuse with Azyr Talent.

## The Lore of Heavens

Damage-dealing Lore of Heavens spells ignore metal Armour Points and arc to strike everyone else within 2 yards (except those with Arcane Magic (Heavens)) for a magic-missile hit equal to Willpower Bonus. Ingredients: astronomical instruments, mirrors, feathers, carved metal.

## NPC: Raphael Julevno, Magister of the Celestial College

A rising prodigy tipped to become High Celestial, Julevno would rather pursue his own research on how Azyr moves against the flow of time than climb the College hierarchy. He experiences constant unbidden visions of possible futures, including a recurring vision of the current High Celestial's murder — a thread GMs can use as an adventure hook, alongside his search for his unknown father and rumours of eastern mages who have mastered Astromancy beyond the Empire's understanding.

**Grand Astromancer (Gold 1):** M 4, WS 38, BS 29, S 31, T 34, I 50, Ag 41, Dex 34, Int 65, WP 67, Fel 33, W 15.
Key Talents: Aethyric Attunement, Arcane Magic (Heavens), Instinctive Diction, Magical Sense, Second Sight, Sixth Sense.
Spells: full Petty and Arcane spell lists, plus most of the Lore of Heavens (including Comet of Casandora, Curse of Fate, Divination, Fate's Fickle Fingers, Starcrossed, Thorsen's Thunderstorm).
Trappings: Sword, Staff topped with Telescope, Library of Prophetic Texts, Collection of Lustrian Gold Plaques.
