# Magical Nemeses & Adventures

Recurring magical villains for a campaign, plus condensed adventure seeds built around them and around the Winds of Magic generally.

## Building a Nemesis

A good recurring villain (a "nemesis") is never dealt with in one session — they, their minions, and their goals resurface across a campaign. Three things make a nemesis work:

- **Power:** able to defeat the Characters through strength or guile — "defeat" can just mean staying one step ahead, not killing them.
- **A Plan:** either a single driving goal (e.g. ascend to Daemonhood) or an ongoing agenda (e.g. collect every spell ever cast) that can fuel a string of adventures.
- **Obsessiveness:** they don't give up after one setback — they try again, adapt, or hatch a new scheme.

Nemeses range from sympathetic-but-dangerous (redeemable, or at least understandable) to irredeemably evil-but-efficient (worth negotiating with only for information). Some are simply too powerful to fight directly early on — let the Characters oppose their agents instead, keeping the nemesis a mysterious background threat.

## Notable Nemeses

### Egrimm van Horstmann

**Motivation:** earn Daemonhood from Tzeentch. **Short-term:** recruit more wizards into his cult, the Cabal. **Long-term:** subvert every magical institution in the Empire.

A former Patriarch of the Light College who secretly practised Chaos sorcery for years before his treachery was exposed; when cornered, he freed the Chaos Dragon Baudros, who burned him and remade his body in Chaos armour in exchange for his soul. He now rules from the Silver Towers in the Screaming Hills, directing the Cabal — wizards he has personally corrupted — to recruit and corrupt further, and infiltrate cults such as the Red Crown and Purple Hand. He is patient, indirect, and prefers manipulation to open conflict.

### The Blue Scribes of Tzeentch

Twin lesser Daemons — P'tarix (writes, cannot read) and Xirat'p (reads, cannot write) — fragments of Tzeentch himself, shattered in an ancient war among the Ruinous Powers. They are compelled to hunt down and record every spell ever cast, in or out of time, riding a flying, book-laden Library Disc. A wizard who is about to create or significantly vary a spell is plagued by flickering blue light and scratching-quill sounds before the Scribes manifest and try to steal the spell from their mind at the moment of casting.

### Mòna Mimn

**Motivation:** make others feel her loneliness. **Short-term:** revive an ancient Waystone she's discovered. **Long-term:** use it to turn the Old World into a vast, cold marsh.

The last Meargh (matriarch) of a long-dead Fimir clan, a thousand years old and driven half-mad by isolation in her bog. She has found the ruins of an Elf Waystone and is slowly reviving it, expanding her marsh outward mile by mile using Fenbeasts she conjures from Beaststones, and searching for more Waystone fragments (or knowledge of them) to complete the ritual. She's dangerous but not irredeemably evil — she doesn't want to kill, she wants an audience, and Characters who recognise her grief-driven motivation may be able to talk her down rather than fight her.

## Adventure Hooks

- **A Man of Influence:** A Jade Magister offers to sponsor the Characters if they help him expose a Nurgle cult — but the cult is real and he's secretly one of van Horstmann's Cabal, using the "test" to recruit the Characters as unwitting pawns.
- **Enough is Enough:** A noble family framed by van Horstmann for spreading plague has gathered his other enemies (including a Light College Patriarch) and wants capable agents to help bring down the Cabal for good.
- **A Night to Remember:** Two wizards preparing a spectacular new spell for a Middenheim festival are unknowingly about to summon the Blue Scribes mid-performance, in front of a huge crowd.
- **The Heist:** A shadowmancer wants to lure the Blue Scribes out deliberately so she can loot their flying library disc, and needs a crew to gather ritual components and act as a distraction.
- **The Auction:** A century-old grimoire of forbidden multi-Wind "Colour Magic," compiled by four vanished apprentices, has resurfaced and is being sold at a secret midnight auction — witch hunters, rival colleges, and criminals all want it.
- **The Apprentice:** A talented village girl who can speak with animals is being recruited by the Jade College for testing, but a necromancer wants her as his apprentice first, and the village has hidden her to protect her.
- **Waystone's Reach:** Rumours of thefts from magical libraries (rubbings of ancient stones, wetland travelogues) lead the Characters toward Mòna Mimn's expanding marsh — and a choice between destroying her or trying to reach the grieving Meargh beneath the madness.
