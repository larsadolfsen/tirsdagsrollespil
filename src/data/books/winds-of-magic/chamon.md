# Chamon & the Lore of Metal

## Overview

Chamon, the Gold Wind, is heavy and dense, attracted to metal (especially gold) much as metal is drawn to a magnet — a pull some blame for gold's ability to incite greed and war in ordinary folk. It settles near the poles (-1 SL to Channelling (Chamon) near the equator) and pools thickly around mines, treasure vaults, and other metal-rich places (+1 SL to Channelling there). Chamon governs transformation and purification — changing a thing's state to meet a need, and stripping impurities to reveal a substance's truest form (the goal of "High Alchemy").

## The Golden Order

Also called Gold Wizards or magisters, they study Alchemical Thaumaturgy under the title Alchemist. Symbol: a soaring eagle (an Ulthuan tradition); robes are glinting yellow, often stained or scorched from lab work. The Gold College — nicknamed "The Foundry" — sits on the River Reik in Altdorf, a grimy jumble of smokestacks, forges, and lead-lined laboratories beneath an interior of gold-laced marble and stained glass. Gold Wizards research blackpowder formulae, True Steel, and a hoped-for "spiritual Al-kahest" that could cure mutation. They have the best public relations of any College, serving as advisors to nobility and the Imperial Court, but are hated by radical groups like PLANT and viewed with mixed feelings by Dwarfs (some suspect a Dwarf affinity for Chamon is being hidden). Patriarch: Balthasar Gelt.

## NPC: Balthasar Gelt

Patriarch of the Golden Order and second only to the Supreme Patriarch in influence. His origins are murky — his official biography (a border-kingdom apothecary's son who studied across Araby, Tilea, and Estalia) is likely fiction; he actually arrived in Marienburg as an apparent con artist whose payment gold turned to lead. Admitted to the College without formal apprenticeship, he rose fast, then vanished into his lab for a mysterious "accident" — emerging masked in gold from head to toe, cold and unreadable, and never removing the mask since. A capable and much-sought patron: he sends agents to retrieve rare or dangerous materials for his research, hunt down renegade magisters, or protect his projects from PLANT and thieves.
