# The Winds at Work

GM reference for how the Winds of Magic shape the world at large: environmental saturation, Storms of Magic, leylines and waystones, and a handful of ready-to-use magical locations with adventure hooks.

## Environmental Saturation

Magic constantly seeps into the land; where it's absorbed faster than it dissipates, an area becomes saturated. Saturation has five levels — **Low, Normal, Heavy, Extreme, Corrupted** — and normally rises by 1 level per year (faster near a Storm of Magic, slower/reversed near leylines feeding the Great Vortex).

Certain Winds dominate by sympathetic geography (Ghyran in forests/farmland, Chamon in mountains, Azyr at sea, Shyish in graveyards/battlefields, etc.) and manifest as **Saturation Effects** in local flora, fauna, and weather — folk nickname the phenomena ("Mother's Bosom" for lush Ghyran regions, "Raven's Pickings" for Shyish deathliness, and so on). Heavy Saturation produces 1–2 such effects, Extreme produces 3–4.

**Casting bonuses:** Heavy Saturation gives +1 SL to Casting Tests using the dominant Lore(s); Extreme gives +2 SL to the dominant Lore(s) and +1 SL to all others; Low Saturation penalises all Lores by –1 SL.

### Environmental Corruption

At Extreme Saturation, an area risks becoming **Corrupted** — a 10% chance each Geheimnisnacht/Hexensnacht (when Morrslieb reaches perigee). The GM chooses whether the corruption is **Chaotic** (rolled twice on a table of effects like Pillars of Skulls, Rain of Blood, Frenzied Animals, Diseased Trees; Chaos casters get +1 SL) or **Necromantic** (Petrified Forest, Vampire Bats, Spirit Hosts, Walking Dead; Dark Magic casters get +1 SL, and unconsecrated dead rise as Zombies/Skeletons).

## Storms of Magic

Rare celestial conjunctions cause localised Storms of Magic that grant +2 SL to all Casting Tests (cumulative with other bonuses) and instantly raise Environmental Saturation by +1 level. Each round, roll d10 for the **Magical Flux** — spells from the indicated Lore automatically benefit from Critical Casting if the Casting Test succeeds:

| d10 | Lore | d10 | Lore |
| :---: | --- | :---: | --- |
| 1 | Beasts | 6 | Life |
| 2 | Death | 7 | Light |
| 3 | Fire | 8 | Shadows |
| 4 | Heavens | 9 | Witch Magic |
| 5 | Metal | 10 | Dark/Chaos Magic |

Every spell cast during a Storm also gains GM-chosen Overcasting Effects (2d10, per the normal Overcasting table), on top of anything the caster chose. Wizards holding an arcane fulcrum may spend a Hard (–20) Channelling Test to pick that round's Flux for the whole region.

## Leylines and Waystones

**Leylines** are channels of magical power spiralling toward the Great Vortex in Ulthuan. **Natural leylines** form along rivers, mountains, and other geography where Winds pool by sympathetic attraction (routed via the mysterious Worldroots or the Geomantic Web); they raise regional Saturation by +1/year. **Planned leylines** are artificial networks of waystones built by Elves, Dwarfs, and the Old Ones to funnel magic toward the Vortex, draining Saturation instead.

Casting near any leyline grants +1 SL. **Waystones** carry one or more properties, which can be applied per-facing:

| Property | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Attraction | Passes magic to the next stone in a line; a broken chain accumulates into a Saturated Leyline Junction. |
| Refraction | Splits raw magic into its 8 colours; +1 SL Channelling for Beasts/Death/Fire/Heavens/Metal/Life/Light/Shadows Lores, –1 SL for others. |
| Dampening | Absorbs excess magic; –2 SL to Casting nearby but +2 SL to Dispel attempts; prevents Saturated Junctions. |
| Containment | Reflects magic back to its source; blocks Saturation/Corruption from crossing (used to pen in Warpstone or Dhar sources). |
| Amplification | +2 SL to Casting nearby; if not on a working leyline, adds +1 Saturation level/year instead. |

**The Great Vortex** drains Saturation by 1 level/year from any region a Planned Leyline crosses. **Nexuses of Power** (leyline junctions, often stone circles) grant spellcasters +1 to +3 SL depending on how many leylines converge; the **Geomantic Web** (an unrelated, mostly-unseen earth-energy grid used by Slann) grants +2 SL to Channelling at its nexus points.

### Corrupted Leylines and Arcane Fulcrums

Damaged waystones turn a leyline into a **Dhar leyline** (a Malignant Influence granting +1 SL to Witchcraft/Dark/Chaos Magic) and eventually pool into a **Dhar Pool**. A disrupted junction can become an **Arcane Fulcrum** — Casting bonus +2 to +8 SL, Critical Casting twice as likely, but Saturation rises +1 level/month until stabilised or (within a year) it becomes a permanent Dhar Pool. **Warp Rifts** (tears where reality is thin) and **Magic Portals** (deliberately opened, single-colour versions of the same) give a random +1 to +5 SL each round (roll d10/2); Warp Rifts count as Malignant Influences and double the Daemons produced by summoning rituals there. On Geheimnisnacht/Hexensnacht, Dhar Pools negate the Unstable Trait and grant +2 SL to Dark/Chaos/Witch casting, while Warp Rifts may spawn extra Daemons.

## Notable Magical Locations

Ready-to-drop sites tied to a Wind of Magic, each with a mechanical effect and a seed for an adventure.

### The Forge of Henoth

A lost Dwarf rune-smithy in the hills above Geetburg, now jointly run by Gold College alchemists and Dwarf Loremasters trying to tame its wild Chamon surges. *Effect:* +2 SL to Casting Lore of Metal spells here, but –2 SL to Channelling Chamon; any Minor Miscast (regardless of Lore) uses the Arcane Marks of Chamon table.
**Hook:** A Gold Wizard accidentally transmuted a drinking calf into "Elsie," a Chamon-charged Minotaur-statted monster that now guards the forge and killed her handler — someone has to deal with it before the Dwarfs return.

### Uli's Tavern and Debating Arena

An Altdorf inn purpose-built to harvest Aqshy from heated debates, which is then piped into an adjoining smithy that arms Bright College magisters. *Effect:* Casting/Channelling Lore of Fire spells here benefit from up to +3 SL depending on how heated the debate is; Flaming Sword of Rhuin can be cast on the smithy's blades at CN 2.
**Hook:** A Dwarf spy is deliberately sabotaging relations between the tavern's smiths and a rival Dwarf hold to redirect a lucrative contract — someone has hired the Characters to find out what's souring things.

### Paranoth's Column

A 67-foot, ivy-choked obelisk outside Eilhart, marking a Ghyran-streaming leyline and the drowned tower of a rival wizard the column's builder once destroyed.
**Hook:** The unquiet spirit of the drowned wizard, Beltran, is manipulating villagers who pray at the column into planning a human sacrifice at the next conjunction of Morrslieb.

### The Shadow Maze

A shifting labyrinth of living Ulgu mist at the heart of the Grim Moor, hiding the ancient, prophecy-inscribed Stones of Barbaneagra and a small camp of Grey Order scholars. *Effect:* +2 SL to Casting/Channelling Lore of Shadows nearby.
**Hook:** A magister who has lived at the stones for six years, terrified of admitting failure to the College, has begun bullying and possibly murdering junior scholars he suspects of learning more than him.

### The High Lodge

A mountain retreat of the Amber Order at the edge of the Drakwald, where shamans ritually transform ("the Binding") volunteers into animals under the power of Ghur. *Effect:* +2 SL to Casting/Channelling Lore of Beasts nearby; within the sacred cave, Lore of Beasts spells have halved CN and doubled Duration, and Beast Form/Wyssan's Wildform can be cast on others.
**Hook:** A Character (or their Beast-touched ally) is summoned or tricked into undergoing the Binding, then owes the Lodge Kin a favour in a nearby town.

### The Vale of the Necromancer

A guarded valley east of Ostermark where the Amethyst Order contains the obsidian "Necromancer's Mirror," a Shyish/Dhar scrying altar that killed the necromancer who first found it. *Effect:* every hour spent in the vale requires a Challenging (+0) Willpower Test or gain a Fatigued Condition (only removable by rest away from the vale or a Resolve Point); the mirror lets Dying Words summon a named spirit, or can be read with a Hard (–20) Psychometry-equivalent Test.
**Hook:** A grieving noble hires the Characters to steal a rumoured resurrection flower from the vale — or the Characters use the Mirror themselves and glimpse a dangerous secret about someone powerful.

### The Cairnapan Complex

A ruined field of stone circles, barrows, and dykes in the Great Forest, soaked in Ghyran and increasingly claimed by a malevolent nature-spirit unleashed by an outlawed spell (Roots of Wrath). *Effect:* CN of Lore of Life rituals is halved in the area.
**Hook:** A hedge witch's apprentice has vanished near the ruins, and a rich noble is separately hunting down barrow relics he believes complete a dangerous set.
