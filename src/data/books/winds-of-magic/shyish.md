# Shyish & the Lore of Death

## Overview

Shyish, the Amethyst Wind, is the most elusive of the Winds, weaving through past, present, and future along the currents of fate. It gathers most strongly wherever death lies heavy — battlefields, graveyards, ruins, gibbets — and is popularly (if inaccurately) equated with Necromancy: Shyish is properly a wind of transition, time, and memory, not just death itself. Channelling (Shyish) Tests gain +1 SL where many bodies are interred or great carnage occurred, and suffer -1 SL in places spared violence and death.

## The Amethyst Order

- **Symbol:** Scythe, Hourglass, Skull, Rose. **Focus:** Death, Time, Endings. **Title:** Brethren, Spiriter.
- Wizards of the Amethyst Order (the Sixth Lore of Magic) wear purple robes, carry a scythe rather than a staff, and study Spirit Lore — contacting and commanding spirits, and dealing death directly. They are shunned by common folk and tainted by association with Necromancy, despite being its committed enemies: the order vigilantly weeds out apprentices showing Necromantic tendencies.
- Their campus is a dark, mausoleum-like building beside Altdorf's Haunted Cemetery. Members rarely socialise even with each other, preferring solitary study, whispered lectures, and (reportedly) telepathy over speech.
- Long exposure to Shyish leaves magisters gaunt, pale, and emotionally withdrawn. The order maintains an unusual alliance with the life-affirming Jade Order (Ghyran), and a tense but cooperative relationship with the Cult of Morr, some of whose hardline priests suspect the Amethyst Wizards of disguised Necromancy.

## Arcane Marks of Shyish

A d10 table of quirks marking heavy Shyish use — withered muscles (-5 Strength), white cataract-like eyes (-1 SL Fellowship), a repellent cold touch, causing accelerated aging in newborns nearby, or an unsettling hoarse whisper of a voice (-1 SL Casting). Rolling a 9 lets you tune into the whispers of the local dead via a Perception Test (risking Corruption on a Fumble); rolling a 10 grants a visible rune and the Suffuse with Shyish Talent.

## The Lore of Death

Any Lore of Death spell may inflict 1 Fatigued Condition on a living target (only one such Condition from this Lore active per target at a time). Ingredients: bones, coffin fragments, grave dirt, hourglasses, silver coins, and purple gems/flowers (especially roses).

## NPC: Elspeth von Draken, Magisterix

A reclusive, immensely powerful sorceress of Nuln who stands halfway between life and death from her long saturation in Shyish. She answers to no one but herself, ignores the Colleges of Magic's summonses, maintains a pact with the Cult of Morr, and commands a bound Carmine Dragon. She is a valuable but demanding potential patron or trainer, hard to approach and unwilling to take on apprentices lightly. Notable magic items in her possession include the **Pale Scythe** (a keening, shadow-wrought weapon) and **Death's Timekeeper** (an ancient hourglass said to hold a dead god's dust, granting her a spare Fortune Point).

**Human Wizard Lord (Gold 2):** M 4, WS 47, BS 32, S 33, T 30, I 53, Ag 53, Dex 37, Int 70, WP 65, Fel 45, W 14. Traits: Ethereal, Immunity to Psychology, Weapon (The Pale Scythe) +7.
Key Talents: Aethyric Attunement, Arcane Magic (Death), Combat Aware, Instinctive Diction 2, Luck 3, Magical Sense, Second Sight, War Wizard.
Spells: Petty (Dart, Drain, Shock, Warning); Arcane (Aethyric Arms, Bridge, Flight, Teleport); has memorised the entire Lore of Death.
