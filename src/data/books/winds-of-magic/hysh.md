# Hysh & the Lore of Light

## Overview

Hysh, the White Wind, is a wind of illumination, healing/protection of the mind, and exorcism — it burns away Chaos and cannot be withstood by Daemons. It is the most elusive and hardest Wind to master; it soaks quickly into the ground and drifts as far as Araby and Nehekhara, where ancient pyramids and obelisks were built to concentrate it.

**A Difficult Wind:** Casting and Channelling Tests using the Lore of Light suffer -1 SL in most conditions; Second Sight Tests (Perception, Intuition, Track) made to perceive Hysh suffer -2 SL. A nearby assistant with Channelling (Hysh) can negate the penalty by chanting: they must pass an Easy (+40) Language (Magick) Test, and the effect lasts as long as they keep chanting aloud.

## The Order of Light

Known as the Light Order, White Wizards, or hierophants, they study Illuminatory Thaumaturgy under the title Hierophant. Symbols: Serpent, Candle, Mirror, Pillar. Robes are brilliant white; a serpentine staff is common. Their college in Altdorf resembles transplanted Nehekharan architecture — alabaster pyramids and a third pyramid made entirely of visible Hysh, maintained by choirs of chanting acolytes. Acolytes are recruited young (often orphans) and uniquely learn to channel magic to feed spells cast by senior wizards, not just their own. The current Patriarch is Vespasian Kant, who exposed his corrupted predecessor Egrimm van Horstmann as a Tzeentch cultist in 2507 IC. Light Wizards are respected by the military and nobility for fighting Chaos, but distrusted by Witch Hunters and treated with wary respect by Elves.

## NPC: Ashamira Dib

A self-taught Arabyan hierophant with the Sight, trained by a mysterious wandering tutor. After Undead forces destroyed her home and followers, she is travelling to Altdorf seeking the Light College's help fighting Necromancers and the Undead of Khemri — pursued along the way by Necromancers, wary Witch Hunters, and suspicious hierophants. She can become a strong ally/patron for characters escorting her, teaching them to identify and fight Necromancers and Undead.
