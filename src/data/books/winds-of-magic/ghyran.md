# Ghyran & the Lore of Life

## Overview

Ghyran, the Jade Wind, is the wind of life itself — found in blood and sap, tied closely to water, and released in a burst at the moment of death (a fact druids keep closely guarded). It settles in idyllic groves, farmland, and tangled wilderness alike, glowing brighter with vitality and duller with disease or decline.

**Seasonal & Environmental Variations:** Channelling (Ghyran) Tests gain +1 SL near abundant water (rivers, lakes, oceans — not mere streams) but suffer -1 SL in dry environments. Because Jade Wizards are tied to the seasons, their power also waxes and wanes: +1 SL during Pflugzeit and Vorgeheim, -1 SL during Ulriczeit and Vorhexen.

## The Jade Order

Known as druids, they study Agrological Thaumaturgy under the title Druid. Symbols: Spiral, Triskele, Oak Tree, Sickle; robes are green. Druids carry a staff of living wood (planted at night to help it grow) and a sickle — copper for apprentices, silver on licensing, gold for senior druids. The Jade College in Altdorf is a walled, entirely living garden-complex grown from tree limbs, with a library housed in a great oak trunk. Few druids stay there long; many maintain hidden forest groves and stone circles elsewhere, or wander seasonally before returning home each autumn. The order supplies battle wizards but feels underused by generals who prefer fire magic. Relations: friendly with the misanthropic Amber wizards and (informally) hedge witches; strained but productive with the Eonir Elves; quietly tense with the Cult of Rhya, who see Jade magic as a tainted shortcut.

## NPC: Tochter Grunfeld

An ageless, shapeshifting-seeming Jade Wizard Lord (sometimes tree-spirit, sometimes young woman, sometimes grandmother) who spent decades in eastern Stirland fighting blight and Vampires before relocating to the Jade College in Altdorf, where she's now a respected senior councillor and likely successor to the current Jade Mother. She founded the commune of Bluetegarten, whose past ties to witches and Rhya cultists still dog her reputation, though it hasn't stopped her rise. She currently runs a spy network watching for vampiric activity in Sylvania and can serve as an information-broker patron, sending characters to investigate Sylvanian threats or handle blackmail attempts tied to her past.

## Lore of Life Spells

Living creatures (without the Construct, Daemonic, or Undead Trait) targeted by Lore of Life spells have all Fatigued and Bleeding Conditions removed after other effects apply. Creatures with the Undead Trait instead suffer extra Damage equal to Willpower Bonus on any damage-dealing spell, ignoring Toughness Bonus/Armour. Ingredients: rare seeds/nuts, tree saps, fertile loam, spring water, living plants or small animals.
