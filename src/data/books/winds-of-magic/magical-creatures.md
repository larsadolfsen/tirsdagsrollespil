# Magical Creatures

Condensed stat blocks for the aethyric creatures of Winds of Magic. These are distinct from Chaos-born Daemons or nature-born beasts: they are things given shape and life by raw magic. Three broad types exist: Incarnate Elementals, Magical Constructs, and Familiars.

## Incarnate Elementals

Powerful, half-sentient beings summoned when a wizard binds the purest, most destructive essence of a Wind of Magic into a spirit-creature. Only three have documented summoning rituals: Fire, Death, and Beasts (summoned respectively by the Bright, Amethyst, and Amber Colleges). Rumours persist of other Incarnates — Life, Light, and more — but none are confirmed.

**New Trait — Grim (Rating):** if the creature does not have at least its Grim Rating in Advantage at the start of its turn, its Advantage pool immediately jumps to that Rating.

### Nature Spirits

A looser catch-all for woodland entities distinct from Incarnate Elementals — Dryads, Naiads, Spites, Treemen, and stranger elder forms (Unicorns, Great Stags, giant toads). They thrive in regions of long-standing magical saturation, predate the Elves, and can be slain despite often appearing incorporeal. No single stat block is given; reskin an appropriate Bestiary entry (e.g. Bear, Giant, Wolf) with Magical, Arboreal, and similar Traits as needed.

## Magical Constructs

Physical bodies animated by magic but possessing no mind of their own — they do exactly what they are told, and only that. Built (not born), they are commissioned as bodyguards, home guardians, labourers, or (rarely, and unreliably) battlefield soldiers, since a construct whose wizard's concentration breaks becomes confused or even turns on allies. See the Create Construct Ritual for the crafting process.

- **Bodyguards:** usually humanoid to avoid drawing attention while escorting a wizard in public.
- **Homeguards:** left to protect a wizard's home/workshop while they are elsewhere; typically disguised as mundane objects, statues, or taxidermy.
- **Labourers:** built for brute physical work (hauling, rowing, mining); wizards keep them out of sight to avoid provoking local guilds.
- **Soldiers:** rare, since constructs need constant direction and can falter or turn on allies when their controlling wizard is disrupted mid-battle.

### Fenbeast

A construct of animated swamp slime and rotting plant matter, built around a carved "heartstone" that draws the Winds of Magic to sustain it; lives only days unless continually reinforced. Common in the Fens of Albion and used as labourers/guards by the Jade College.

## Familiars

Small, intelligent magical servants created via the Create Familiar Ritual, formed from a portion of their creator's own vitality (the wizard surrenders a point of Fate or Resilience to make one). Unlike constructs, familiars are self-aware, have their own personalities (usually flavoured by the Wind that made them), and remain loyal — if sometimes grudgingly — to their creator. There are three types: **Combat Familiars** (bodyguards), **Power Familiars** (assist casting/research, cannot themselves cast), and **Spell Familiars** (magicians in their own right, learn up to four spells from their creator). Most spellcasters can only create the type(s) tied to their Lore; Necromancers and Daemonologists typically produce small Undead or Daemonic familiars instead.

### Familiar Rules Quick Reference

- Familiars advance via the same Skill/Talent/Characteristic Advance system as other characters, but can never change career from their familiar type, and gain no Trappings beyond what's listed.
- They are not paid, have no Status, and suffer Hard (–20) Fellowship Tests from strangers (most people are unnerved by them).
- They take damage, heal, and are affected by Corruption/Mutation like other characters, but non-magical medicine (including the Heal Skill and healing miracles) does not work on them — only magical healing does.
- They don't need food/water unless animal in origin, and don't suffer Bleeding/Fatigued/Poisoned/disease unless the source was magical.
- **Suffuse With (Wind)** (new Talent, Max 1): grants a lore-specific perk (e.g. Aqshy familiars can enter Frenzy off Channelling; Ulgu familiars use Channelling in place of Stealth) and a passive +1 SL aura for same-Lore spells cast within 8 yards.
