# Ulgu & the Lore of Shadows

## Overview

Ulgu, the Grey Wind, is a heavy, sluggish fog of deception, illusion, and secrets. It gathers wherever people are deceived, objects concealed, or plots hatched — dark alleyways, dim palace corridors, foggy swamps — and is said to cloak whole secrets from society at large (folklore claims it hides the very existence of the Fimir and the Skaven). Channelling (Ulgu) Tests benefit from +1 SL in stormy or foggy weather, and suffer -1 SL on clear sunny days.

## The Grey Order

- **Symbol:** Sword, Cowl, Cloud. **Focus:** Deception, Illusion, Confusion, Darkness. **Title:** Grey Guardian, Shadowmancer.
- Wizards of the Grey Order (the Fifth Lore of Magic) wear grey robes and are the most secretive and distrusted of all the Colleges, known in scorn as "Trickster wizards."
- Their college is a shabby, unassuming building in Altdorf's poorest district, hiding a much larger underground complex reached by secret passages throughout the city.
- Grey Wizards rarely settle at the college, instead travelling the Empire (often in disguise, holding second identities) to gather intelligence. They work closely with Imperial spy networks such as the Black Chamber, and are viewed coldly by the truth-valuing Cult of Verena.
- Apprentices train by shadowing a single master on the road rather than attending formal classes, forging an intimate but insular mentor-apprentice bond. The order forbids members from seeking personal power or political office, seeing such ties as compromising their freedom — Magisters Vigilant enforce this internally, and offenders "vanish without a trace."

## Arcane Marks of Ulgu

A d10 table of quirks marking heavy Ulgu use — a hesitant, obfuscating manner of speech (-1 SL Charm/Gossip), an affinity for mist and fog (+1 SL Stealth in haze, -1 SL in bright light), grey swirling eyes with Night Vision, an unruly shadow that unsettles onlookers, or becoming forgettably generic-looking (gain Beneath Notice). Rolling a 10 grants a visible rune and the Suffuse with Ulgu Talent.

## The Lore of Shadows

Spells cast from this Lore are surreptitious, and any protective spell you cast wreathes you in shadow and smoke, granting +20 Stealth for its duration. All damage-dealing Lore of Shadows spells ignore non-magical Armour Points. Ingredients: cosmetics, scents, mirrors, wigs, diplomatic trinkets, blades.

## NPC: Immanuel-Ferrand Holswig-Schliestein

A powerful royal spymaster and uncle of the Emperor, holding the office of High Chancellor of the Realm and heading the Graukappen secret service. He is a member of the Grey Order but no master of Ulgu, and currently finds himself politically compromised after being manoeuvred into a disastrous political blunder by rivals in the Black Chamber. Useful as a patron whose intrigues (and enemies, including a rival being groomed to replace him) can draw a party into court politics.

**Human Spymaster (Gold 4):** M 4, WS 59, BS 31, S 55, T 55, I 57, Ag 58, Dex 38, Int 64, WP 59, Fel 64, W 14.
