# Ghur & the Lore of Beasts

## The Amber Order

The Amber College studies Ghur, the Wind of Beasts, and its wizards are called shamans. They are solitary, feral, and plainspoken, favouring the wilderness over civilisation and beasts over people. Uniquely among the Colleges, the Amber Order keeps no building in Altdorf — its Masters instead inhabit caves in the Amber Hills and similar hidden sites across the Empire.

- **Symbol**: the Arrow (also claw/feather/tooth), sacred to Taal; shamans wear talismans of teeth, claws, and feathers.
- **Robes**: brown; in practice, weatherworn wilderness gear rather than true robes.
- Ghur resists urban settings: Channelling (Ghur) suffers -1 SL in towns, -2 SL in cities, but gains +1 SL in wilderness and +2 SL in truly wild places. Middenheim is a notable exception (+2 SL), thanks to a Wellspring of Ghur beneath the Fauschlag rock.

### The Shaman Career

Human career progressing Shaman's Apprentice (Brass 3) -> Shaman (Silver 3) -> Master Shaman (Gold 1) -> Shaman Lord (Gold 2).

- **Key skills**: Channelling (Ghur), Animal Care/Training, Charm Animal, Outdoor Survival, Track, Ride.
- **Key Talents**: Petty Magic and Second Sight at Apprentice; Arcane Magic (Lore of Beasts) from Shaman; Animal Affinity and Instinctive Diction at Master; War Wizard at Lord.
- **Trappings**: a Grimoire often scribed on bone or teeth, a Staff of wood or bone hung with totems; no formal workshop is needed to advance — any natural shelter (tree, cave) serves.

## Ghur, the Amber Wind

Ghur is a cold, savage wind that flows from wild places, accumulating in forests, mountains, swamps, and moors, and around great predators. It appears as a muddy amber-brown haze, tending toward yellow where it blows harshly and deep brown where entrenched.

## NPC: Gregor Martak

A wandering Shaman Lord (Gold 2) cursed with vivid, often apocalyptic premonitions about threats to the Empire — Chaos incursions, Beastmen risings, and his own likely rise to Amber patriarch. Terse and solitary but capable of great warmth among those he trusts, Martak makes a strong patron: he sends allies to investigate omens (a corrupted standing stone in Eonir territory, strange Beastmen beneath Empire cities, the terrain of a foretold Chaos Wastes invasion), rewarding loyalty with his considerable, if unpredictable, connections.

### Beast Form: GM Rulings

- A wizard may declare a shorter self-imposed Duration for Beast Form/Transformation of Kadon at the moment of casting, ending the transformation early.
- Transforming ruins worn clothing and drops trappings, except Enchanted Staffs and robes made for Amber Wizards, which transform along with the wizard.
- Critical Injuries suffered in Beast Form carry over to the wizard's true form (GM adapts the location sensibly).
- Skills and Talents are retained in Beast Form unless stated otherwise, though many are impractical without hands or a voice.
- Characteristic Advances are ignored in Beast Form — the creature's base profile is used instead, though Skill Advances still apply.
