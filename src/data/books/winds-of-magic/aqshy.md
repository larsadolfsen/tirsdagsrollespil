# Aqshy & the Lore of Fire

## The Bright Order

The Bright College studies Aqshy, the Wind of Fire, calling their Lore Pyromancy. Bright Wizards are the Empire's premier war wizards, favouring destructive, spectacular magic over subtlety, and are the college most closely tied to the military.

- **Symbol**: the Key of Secrets (also flame/torch); wizards carry a bunch of keys marking rank, up to seven at Pyromancer Lord.
- **Robes**: blazing red and orange.
- **College seat**: an austere red-brick complex in Altdorf, surrounded by scorched ground; a communal domed chamber vents constant smoke.
- Bright Wizards gain +1 SL to Channelling (Aqshy) near a significant fire (bonfire, burning building), +2 SL near a great conflagration (active volcano, a burning town).

### The Pyromancer Career

Human career progressing Apprentice Pyromancer (Brass 3) -> Pyromancer (Silver 3) -> Master Pyromancer (Gold 1) -> Pyromancer Lord (Gold 2).

- **Key skills**: Channelling (Aqshy), Intimidate, Melee (Basic/Polearm), Lore (Magic/Warfare), Charm, Leadership.
- **Key Talents**: Petty Magic and Second Sight at Apprentice; Arcane Magic (Lore of Fire) from Pyromancer; Instinctive Diction and Magical Sense at Master; War Wizard at Lord.
- **Trappings**: Grimoire, Staff, and successive Keys of Secrets; Magical Licence and increasingly elaborate Robes as rank rises.

## Aqshy, the Red Wind

Aqshy is a hot, dry wind drawn to fire, heat, passion, and anger; it is easier to channel by day and in warmth, and gathers around hearths, forges, and battle. Its magic manifests as a red, shimmering haze; its aftermath leaves a scorched, smoky atmosphere.

## NPC: Sergov Pfeiffer

A rambunctious, talented but undisciplined Bright Wizard (Silver 3) who prefers Altdorf's Street of One Hundred Taverns to serious study, while secretly working toward a devastating signature spell ("The Fury of Sigmar"). Useful as a patron who can shield the party from minor law trouble and open doors reserved for wizards, in exchange for research errands, book retrieval, or investigating his own past magical mishaps (e.g. a fire elemental he accidentally created).

## The Lore of Fire
